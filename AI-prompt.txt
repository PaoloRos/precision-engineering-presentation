[Dynamic Selection]

Regarding the Hinge Optimization:

The mechanism design focused not only on implementing different concepts, but also on optimizing the flexure hinges to reduce the overall stiffness while remaining within the valid operating range. Parametric studies and optimization procedures were developed to identify the most suitable hinge geometries for the application.

Several hinge types were initially analysed, including circular, leaf-spring, cartwheel, and elliptical hinges, using analytical models from the literature.

The optimization process was guided by the following criteria:

* Maximize allowable flexure angle
* Minimize hinge stiffness
* Respect analytical-model validity and small-deformation assumptions
* Avoid material yielding due to stress concentrations
* Maintain manufacturability

The final design employed only leaf-spring and circular hinges. Leaf-spring hinges were used in regions with small angular deflections because of their low stiffness, while circular hinges were adopted where higher motion accuracy and larger rotations were required due to their approximately fixed pivot point. This compromise enabled a balance between structural compliance and precise motion guidance.

Overall, the optimization procedure improved the final design and provided insight into practical engineering trade-offs and iterative design processes.
