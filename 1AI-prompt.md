[Slide: Dynamic Design]

Describe the advantages of elastic deformation and the use of flexure hinges.

Regarding hinge optimization:

Different hinge geometries were analysed through analytical models and parametric studies.

The optimization criteria included:

* maximizing allowable flexure angle,
* minimizing stiffness,
* avoiding material yielding,
* maintaining manufacturability,
* respecting analytical-model validity.

The final design employed leaf-spring and circular hinges to balance compliance, precision, and rotational capability.

Emphasize that the optimization procedure improved both the final design quality and the engineering understanding of practical design trade-offs.
